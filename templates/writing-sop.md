# Writing SOP — 7-Layer Framework

Every post follows this structure. Public-domain framework adapted from long-form storytelling principles.

---

## Layer 1 — Hook (1-2 lines)
**Goal:** Stop the scroll in 1.5 seconds.

Rules:
- Max 12 words per line
- No vague claims ("Here's a thought")
- Specific > general (numbers, names, places)
- Trigger curiosity, contradiction, or emotion

Example:
> "I almost fired my best client last Tuesday."
> "Best decision I made this year."

---

## Layer 2 — Context (under 80 words)
**Goal:** Drop the reader into the scene.

Rules:
- ONE specific moment (not summary)
- Concrete details (when, where, who)
- No backstory, no setup
- Show don't tell

Example:
> "He paid $8K/month for 14 months. Always on time. Always polite. Then he forwarded my deliverable to his CMO with the line, 'see if this is worth it.' I read that and felt the floor drop."

---

## Layer 3 — Emotion (150-200 words)
**Goal:** Make the reader feel something specific.

Rules:
- Alternate internal (thoughts/feelings) + external (actions/dialogue)
- Sensory details (sound, sight, body sensations)
- Avoid abstract emotions ("I was sad") in favor of physical ("my chest tightened")
- Vulnerability invites engagement

Example:
> "I closed the laptop. Walked to the kitchen. Stared at the kettle for 4 minutes without turning it on..."

---

## Layer 4 — Value (150-200 words)
**Goal:** Deliver YOUR opinion + the contrast.

Rules:
- Common belief vs your truth
- Evidence (numbers, examples, lived experience)
- Take a position (not "it depends")
- Frame the lesson without preaching

Example:
> "Most agencies will tell you to fight for the client. Send the win-back email. Lower the price. I think that's backwards. The client who tests your worth in private is already gone..."

---

## Layer 5 — Rehook (~50 words at the 60% mark)
**Goal:** Pull the reader back if attention is fading.

Rules:
- One sentence that reframes what they just read
- Or pose the question they're now asking
- Whitespace before and after for visual reset

Example:
> "But here's the part nobody tells you about firing a client."

---

## Layer 6 — CTA (20-40 words)
**Goal:** Invite conversation, not a transaction.

Rules:
- Open-ended question
- Match the post's emotional energy
- Avoid "comment X for the link" tricks
- Avoid "DM me" unless the post earned it

Examples:
- "Have you ever fired a client who paid? What changed for you after?"
- "Curious — does this match what you're seeing in your space?"

---

## Layer 7 — Format (whitespace + flow)

Rules:
- Paragraphs under 40 words (most under 25)
- Break every 1-2 sentences for whitespace
- Total post: 800-1200 words
- No bullet points unless content is genuinely a list
- No headers (LinkedIn doesn't render them well in feed)
- No emojis at start of lines (looks templated)

---

## Anti-Patterns (BANNED — auto-fail)

Skill scans for these. If any present, rewrite + rescan:

- Em dashes (—) — use comma, period, or rewrite
- "It's not about X, it's about Y."
- Parallel triplet fragments: "Same price. Same quality. Same her."
- Stock corporate words: revolutionary, game-changing, skyrocket, leverage, unlock, unleash, dive deep
- "At the end of the day"
- "Buckle up / hot take / unpopular opinion"
- Sycophantic openers ("Great question," "Love this")
- Lists that always have exactly 3 items (vary the count)
- Paragraphs all the same length (vary length)
- Zero contractions (humans use don't / can't / won't)
- Zero hedging ("kinda", "I think", "honestly") — humans hedge

---

## Required human signals (must have at least 2)

- Self-correction ("Actually, scratch that —" or "Wait, that's not right.")
- Specific numbers (not "a lot" but "47 emails")
- Hedging ("I think", "maybe", "probably")
- Casual phrases ("look,", "honestly,", "real talk")
- Mid-thought reset (one short fragment after a long sentence)
