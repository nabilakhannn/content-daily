# Hook Swipe File

Public-domain hook patterns. Skill picks fresh ones each post + checks posts-log to avoid repeats.

You can add your own hooks at the bottom over time.

## 7 Universal Hook Archetypes

### 1. Contrarian / Shock
"[Common belief] is wrong."
"Everyone tells you X. They're lying."
"The thing nobody admits about [topic]."

### 2. Story Start (in-medias-res)
"3 months ago, I [specific thing]."
"Last Tuesday, a client said something that broke me."
"I almost quit yesterday. Here's why I didn't."

### 3. Data / Stat
"[Specific %] of [group] do [thing]. Here's why."
"Studied [N] [examples]. Found [insight]."
"[Year] data: [surprising stat]."

### 4. Authority Statement
"After [N] years doing [thing], here's what I know:"
"I've [done specific impressive thing]. The pattern is:"
"[Title/credential] talking — [statement]."

### 5. Open Question
"What if [common assumption] is the bottleneck?"
"Why do [group] keep [doing thing]?"
"Is [popular thing] actually [opposite]?"

### 6. Confession / Vulnerable
"I was wrong about [topic]."
"I lost [thing] because I believed [thing]."
"Embarrassing truth: [confession]."

### 7. Observation
"Noticed something this week:"
"Sat in [N] [meetings/calls/places] and saw a pattern:"
"Three things changed about [topic] in the last 6 months."

## Lara Pattern Library — Advanced Hook Structures

### 1. Credibility-Before-Claim Hook
Lead with proof, then make the statement.

Pattern: `[Volume/specific experience] + [Surprising finding]`

- "I took on 100+ new clients in 90 days. Three things I thought were true were all wrong."
- "I've reviewed 200+ LinkedIn profiles this year. Most people are making the same 4 mistakes."
- "Built 8 voice agents in the last 90 days. The pricing model you think works doesn't."
- "I've had 3 clients hit $10K+ months in their first 60 days. None of them started with an audience."

---

### 2. Hypothesis-Busting Hook
State 3 widely-held beliefs. Then destroy them. Works especially for contrarian educational posts.

Pattern: `[Belief 1] → [Belief 2] → [Belief 3] → "All 3 were wrong."`

- "I thought you needed 10K followers to get clients. You need to post every day or disappear. LinkedIn is too saturated now. I was wrong about all three."
- "I thought AI tools were just for writing posts. That you needed a big budget to automate. That non-technical founders couldn't build real systems. None of that is true."
- "I thought personal branding required years of content. That coaches needed to niche down before posting. That LinkedIn was only for corporate people. Tested all three. All wrong."

---

### 3. Contrast Proof Hook
Two people. Different contexts. Same result. Proves the mechanism, not the person.

Pattern: `[Person A] has [X]. [Person B] has [Y]. Both [got same result]. The variable is [principle].`

- "One of my clients has been in business for 10 years. Another started 4 months ago. Both are booking discovery calls from LinkedIn. The difference isn't experience. It's how they show up."
- "Greg runs a fintech firm. Alex runs a local gym. One post from each. Both generated inbound leads within 48 hours. Same method. Different industries. The method is what transferred."

---

### 4. Anti-Feature Hook
Name what the result does NOT require. Makes the claim more believable because it removes the objection preemptively.

Pattern: `[Result] — without [common assumption 1], without [common assumption 2], without [common assumption 3]`

- "First 3 discovery calls booked. No cold outreach. No engagement pods. No paid ads."
- "Built a full content system in a weekend. No code. No dev team. No expensive subscriptions."
- "Grew to 10K followers in 6 months. No viral hacks. No daily posting. No LinkedIn premium."

---

### 5. Result-First Hook (Work Backward)
Lead with the outcome. Then make them wonder how.

Pattern: `[Specific result]. [How that feels]. [Reveal what made it possible]`

- "I had two $5K days last week. I didn't pitch anyone."
- "Got 3 inbound leads yesterday. Posted once this week."
- "Client booked their first $8K contract through LinkedIn. They had 340 followers."

---

### 6. Time Compression Hook
Show how long something *used to take* vs how long it takes now.

Pattern: `[Old time] for [task]. Now [new time]. Here's what changed.`

- "3 months to figure out what to post. Now 45 minutes from idea to done. One framework changed everything."
- "Used to spend 6 hours writing a proposal. Now 40 minutes. Same quality. Better close rate."

## 4-Component Alignment (visual content / shorts)

If post pairs with image or short video, align:
- **Visual:** what eye sees
- **Audio/text:** what mouth says
- **Hook:** what brain hears in first 1.5s
- **Promise:** what they get if they keep reading

All 4 must agree. Mismatch = scroll.

---

## Anti-Patterns (BANNED)

These read as AI. Never use:

- "It's not just X, it's Y."
- "Here's why."  (alone as hook)
- "Game-changer / revolutionary / disruptive"
- "Skyrocket / unleash / leverage / dive deep"
- "Buckle up / hot take / unpopular opinion"
- "10 reasons why [topic] is dead"  (overused)
- "Day [N] of [N] doing [thing]"  (overused)
- Triplet endings: "Same X. Same Y. Same Z."
- Sycophantic openers: "Great question."
